# 🏥 CarePulse AI - Clinical Health Vault

CarePulse AI is a comprehensive, modern, and interactive patient healthcare dashboard designed to monitor vital health metrics, track medical records, and manage appointments with AI-assisted clinical insights.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **📊 Vitals & Health Summary Dashboard**:
  - **Real-Time Vitals**: Track Heart Rate, Blood Pressure, Oxygen Level (SpO2), and Blood Sugar levels[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
  - **Overall Health Score**: Dynamic score representation with monthly improvement insights[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
- **🤖 AI Nurse Maya**: Integrated AI health assistant for patient assistance and queries[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
- **📅 Upcoming Appointments**: Manage and view scheduled consultations with doctors[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
- **📂 Health Records & Reports**: Easy access to medical history, lab reports, and digital prescriptions[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
- **👥 Family Members Management**: Multi-profile switching to manage family healthcare[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
- **💬 Messaging & Notifications**: Stay connected with healthcare providers[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
